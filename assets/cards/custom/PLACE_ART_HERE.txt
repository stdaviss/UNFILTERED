Drop replacement PNG, SVG, or WebP files in this folder, then map them in js/data/art-overrides.js:

  export const ART_OVERRIDES = {
    "truth-001": "assets/cards/custom/movie-genre.png"
  };

Character-wide swaps: replace assets/characters/<name>.svg
Avatar swaps: replace assets/avatars/<name>.svg
Card back: replace assets/cards/back.svg
Background: replace assets/backgrounds/office.svg
