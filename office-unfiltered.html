How scoring works

  • Answer a card — 1 point, just for playing it.
  • Table votes — each "too real" or "worth it" vote adds 1 point.
  • Unredacted — everything you scored on that card doubles.
  • Dare beaten on the clock — 2 bonus points.
  • Skip — 0 points, no penalty, no explanation. Skips earn you the Boundaries award at the end.

The six card types

  • Truth — a question with a defined task and a time limit.
  • Dare — a performance. Timer optional.
  • Scenario — a workplace situation the whole table rules on.
  • Archetype — you take a role and a constraint for your next card.
  • Curveball — a rule that bends the next few turns for everyone.
  • Safety — a mechanic that gives control back to the room.
