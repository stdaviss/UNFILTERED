How to play

A workplace social card game. Laughter, recognition, participation — never forced vulnerability.

1. Create or join

The host taps Create Game and shares a short code (OU-4827) or invite link. Nobody needs an account for the MVP.

2. Ready up

Ideal table is 4–8 people. Minimum 3 ready players. Host starts when the room feels right.

3. Take a turn

Current player reveals a card, answers or performs, the group reacts, then the turn ends. Default order is join order.

4. Skip is power

Every player can Skip, Replace, Reframe, ask for a Group Response, Pause, Lower Energy, or End Game. No explanation. No score penalty.

Card types

Truth

Answer a workplace observation. Not a confession.

Dare

A short, optional, physically safe task. Sit, stand, speak, imagine.

Scenario

Discuss a fictional situation. There is no correct answer.

Archetype

Hold a perspective for the next response — The Big-Picture Thinker, The Listener, and friends.

Safety

Overrides normal flow: skip, reframe, pause, lower energy, or close with appreciation.

Scoring

Default can be playful points for finishing a turn. Never for personal disclosure, embarrassment, or risk-taking.

House rules

  • This is a game, not therapy and not an employee review.
  • Keep real names and identifiable incidents out of answers.
  • No sexualized, humiliating, or dangerous tasks.
  • If technology fights comfort, comfort wins.
